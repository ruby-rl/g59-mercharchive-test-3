This folder needs a few small image files for the homepage to look right:

  logo.jpg           - your circular logo image
  icon-instagram.png - Instagram icon
  icon-x.png         - X (Twitter) icon
  icon-link.png      - the "link" icon used for the G59 shop button

Grab these from your current Google Site (right-click each icon -> Save
Image As), rename them to match the list above, and drop them straight in
this assets/img/ folder. Delete this text file once that's done.
